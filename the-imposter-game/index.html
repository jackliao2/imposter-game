Play / What it is

The imposter game is a parlour word game for three or more people. A secret noun is dealt to the crew. One player, the imposter, does not get it. Everyone then writes on a shared phone. The writing is the game: two clues each, left on the table, so the vote can point at a slip instead of at a memory.

People phrase the search as imposter the game, or the game imposter, when they want the activity itself and not a character skin. Both mean this round. It is not a hidden role in a shooter, and it is not a video where someone is voted off a set. If the question is what you do on a Tuesday with one phone, you deal, you write, you vote.

What you are trying to do

Crew want clues that prove they know the word and still do not hand it over. The imposter wants to sound like those clues before the shape of the word is obvious. A caught imposter may still name the word and steal the round. That last guess is why bluffing all the way to the vote can still lose if you never learned the noun.

Questions

What is the imposter game?

A party word game. One player does not know the secret word. Everyone writes two clues that stay on the table, then the group votes for the slip that does not fit.

Is the imposter the game, or a person?

The imposter is the player without the word. The imposter the game refers to is the whole round, not a costume or a role in a shooter.
