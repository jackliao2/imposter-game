A word game on one phone

The imposter game here is a party word game. Everyone sees the same secret except one player. Each person writes a word on the phone, the slips stay on the table, and the group votes for the slip that does not belong. It is free, it runs in the browser, and the longer notes live on their own pages so this one can stay the table.
