How to play the imposter game

The imposter game is a party word game for three or more people around one phone. Everyone is shown the same secret word except one player. That player is the imposter. You go around the table and each person says a single word about the secret. Then you vote, in secret, on who was bluffing.

The group wins only when one imposter is the only person with the most votes. A tie lets the imposter slip away. If the imposter was caught, they still get one guess at the word. Name it and they steal the round.

One phone, then the talk

Pass the phone. The screen tells you whose turn it is to look. Press and hold to see the card, let go, and the word is gone before the next person takes it. Both spellings, imposter and impostor, are the same game. Say one clue. Do not define the word, and do not name the category.

Classic or lookalike

Classic is the imposter game most tables mean: the imposter sees that they are the imposter, and everyone else sees the word. Lookalike gives the imposter a cousin of the word, like tea when the table has coffee, and does not tell them. The clues get slippery, which is the better game once your group already knows the classic.
