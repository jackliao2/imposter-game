Play / Decks / Work deck

Work is a deck of titles. The uniform and the workplace are the two fastest ways to lose. A hospital clue covers doctor, nurse, surgeon, midwife, and paramedic at once, then the second pass has to do all the work. Deal above is already this deck. The room mix, and the other eight, are listed on decks.

Clues that survive a pass

Tools are better than buildings, and still dangerous if the tool is unique. Keys, for locksmith, is the definition. Use the moment of the job instead.

Baton separates conductor from the musicians, who are also in the deck. Two passes still apply. If the first word was wide on purpose, the second has to risk something or the vote has no evidence.
