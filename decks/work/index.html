Play / Decks / Work deck

Work is a deck of titles. The uniform and the workplace are the two fastest ways to lose. A hospital clue covers doctor, nurse, surgeon, midwife, and paramedic at once, then the second pass has to do all the work. Deal above is already this deck. Clues that survive a pass
