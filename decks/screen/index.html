Play / Decks / Screen deck

Screen is films and series, dealt as titles. The trap is the genre. Animated, scary, and sitcom sort the deck into piles the imposter can live inside. Deal above is already this deck. Sports broadcasts are not in it. Those words are on the sports deck.

Clues that survive a pass

Naming the lead actor is usually the title in disguise. A prop or a place from the story lasts longer, and the second written word is where you risk the detail that makes a vote possible.

Questions

Are these words really in the deck?

Yes. The screen deck deals titanic, frozen, the office, bluey, spirited away, jaws. The rows are a sample of that list.

Can we play this deck as lookalike?

No. Lookalike does not deal titanic. This page stays on the screen list.
