Play / Decks / Class deck

Class is the deck for a mixed-age table and for a classroom that asked first. The words are objects and routines, not dares. Teacher is a weak clue because half the deck sits near one. Deal above is already this deck. The room mix, and the other eight, are listed on decks.

Clues that survive a pass

Subject names, such as math or history, only help when the word is the subject. On an object they are a shrug.

If the first word was wide on purpose, the second has to risk something or the vote has no evidence. The counting and the steal guess are on how to play.

Questions

Are these words really in the deck?

Yes. The table deals from this list. The sample is a handful of them, not the whole deck, so a later round is not spoiled.

Can we play this deck as lookalike?

Lookalike uses cousin pairs instead of a subject deck. Switch the mode on the table, or open the lookalike page.
