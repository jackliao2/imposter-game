Play / Decks / Supper deck

Supper is the easiest deck to start and the easiest deck to ruin. Everyone reaches for the taste. Taste-words are how the imposter joins the trail on the first pass. Deal above is already this deck. The room mix, and every other subject deck, are listed on decks.

Clues that survive a pass

Do not write the meal the word belongs to. Breakfast, snack, and dinner sort the deck into three piles and the imposter only has to pick a pile.

Iron is doing a lot of work for waffle: it can mean the press, and it can mean the griddle. Pit for olive is better than green, because green also fits grape, which is in this deck.

Written clues remain on the supper felt, voters mark a slip, and one guess steals the round if the dish is named.

Questions

Are these words really in the deck?

Yes. The food deck deals waffle, soup, popcorn, cinnamon, dumpling, olive. The rows are a sample of that list.

Can we play this deck as lookalike?

No. Lookalike does not deal waffle. This page stays on the food list.
