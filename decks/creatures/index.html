Play / Decks / Creatures deck

Creatures punishes the word pet, the word zoo, and the habitat said too early. Farm, ocean, and jungle are sorting hats. Say one and the imposter is already in the right third of the deck. Deal above is already this deck. The room mix, and every other subject deck, are listed on decks.

Clues that survive a pass

Size is almost as bad as habitat. Huge, on elephant or whale, is a confession. Use a habit or a body part that is not the mascot.

Ceiling is a gecko clue because of how they stand on walls. Cube is the wombat's droppings, which is memorable and not the word itself. Skip it for a young table and use burrow instead, knowing burrow is wider. Two passes still apply. If the first word was wide on purpose, the second has to risk something or the vote has no evidence. Questions

Are these words really in the deck?

Yes. The animals deck deals otter, gecko, peacock, jellyfish, wombat, falcon. The rows are a sample of that list.

Can we play this deck as lookalike?

No. Lookalike does not deal otter. This page stays on the animals list.
