Play / Decks / Weather deck

Weather is sky, plants, and ground. Hot, cold, and wet are true of a third of the deck. They are polite clues and they do not help a vote. Deal above is already this deck. The room mix, and every other subject deck, are listed on decks.

Clues that survive a pass

Season names are the habitat problem again. Winter on icicle is almost the word.

Clock is the waiting between geyser bursts. Wish is the seed head, not the petal. After is the gap between lightning and thunder, and it only works if the table is willing to be oblique.

Both written clues remain posted in a weather round, the vote names a slip, and one guess steals the round if that sky or ground word is named.

Questions

Are these words really in the deck?

Yes. The nature deck deals icicle, cactus, geyser, driftwood, dandelion, thunder. The rows are a sample of that list.

Can we play this deck as lookalike?

No. Lookalike does not deal icicle. This page stays on the nature list.
