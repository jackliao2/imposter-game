Play / Decks

All mixes every list. The subject decks, including sports and movies, are for a table that wants one field. Each deck page lists words that are actually dealt, with a first clue that ends the round and a first clue that leaves the imposter work to do. Open the deck, then press Deal. The table above follows the page you are on.

  • Food: dishes and drinks, not the category name.
  • Animals, where "pet" is almost always too close.
  • Places you travel to.
  • Home: objects within arm's reach.
  • Nature: sky, plants, and ground.
  • Jobs. Titles are easy to give away.
  • Sports and the gear around them.
  • School, for a younger table or a loud one.
  • Clothes. "Wear" is not a clue.
  • Movies and series. The genre is too wide a first word.

Lookalike ignores these decks and uses cousin pairs instead. That mode is explained on lookalike.

Whichever deck is open, both written clues remain in view, you cast a vote for a slip, and one guess steals the round the same way on every list.

Questions

Can we choose two decks at once?

Pick one, or stay on All. All already mixes every list.
