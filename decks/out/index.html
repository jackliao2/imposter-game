Play / Decks / Out deck

Out is travel, buildings, and the bits of town you stand in. The failure mode is naming the trip. Vacation, city, and abroad tell the imposter nothing useful and tell the crew even less, so the vote becomes a guess. Deal above is already this deck. The room mix, and every other subject deck, are listed on decks.

Clues that survive a pass

Proper place names are too close. Paris for vineyard, or London for clock tower, shrinks the word to a postcard.

Spiral is the stair. Coins are the machines that still want them. Ashes is the fire ring, and it is safer than tent, which is almost the definition of campsite.

Both written clues remain in view during an out round, voting selects a slip, and one guess steals the round if the place is named.

Questions

Are these words really in the deck?

Yes. The places deck deals lighthouse, laundromat, vineyard, pier, campsite, clock tower. The rows are a sample of that list.

Can we play this deck as lookalike?

No. Lookalike does not deal lighthouse. This page stays on the places list.
