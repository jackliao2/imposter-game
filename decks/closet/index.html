Play / Decks / Closet deck

Closet is what you put on. Wear, clothes, and outfit are not clues. They are the name of the deck, and the rules ask you not to name it. Deal above is already this deck. The room mix, and every other subject deck, are listed on decks.

Clues that survive a pass

Warm and cold sort the deck into seasons and then stop. A second seasonal clue, such as snow after parka, ends it.

Penny is the slot on a loafer. Bib is the front of the overalls. Pair is sly for mittens because gloves are also in the deck and also come in pairs, so the imposter cannot treat it as a fingerprint.

Both written clues remain readable beside the closet names, the ballot chooses a slip, and one guess steals the round when the garment is named.

Questions

Are these words really in the deck?

Yes. The clothes deck deals beret, parka, loafers, mittens, bow tie, overalls. The rows are a sample of that list.

Can we play this deck as lookalike?

No. Lookalike does not deal beret. This page stays on the clothes list.
