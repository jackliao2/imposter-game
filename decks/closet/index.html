Play / Decks / Closet deck

Closet is what you put on. Wear, clothes, and outfit are not clues. They are the name of the deck, and the rules ask you not to name it. Deal above is already this deck. The room mix, and the other eight, are listed on decks.

Clues that survive a pass

Warm and cold sort the deck into seasons and then stop. A second seasonal clue, such as snow after parka, ends it.

Two passes still apply. If the first word was wide on purpose, the second has to risk something or the vote has no evidence. The counting and the steal guess are on how to play.
