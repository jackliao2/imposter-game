Play / How to play

Three or more people, one phone. Everyone but the imposter sees the same secret word. You write clues onto the table, then you vote for the slip that does not belong. The group wins only when a single imposter has strictly the most votes. A tie lets the imposter stay in. A caught imposter still gets one typed guess, and a correct guess steals the round.

The deal

Tap Deal. The phone names whose card it is. That person presses and holds. Crew see the word. In a blank-card game the imposter sees a red seal and no word. Let go before you pass the phone. The card face is the same paper for everyone, so a glance from across the room does not show who flinched.

Two passes

The first word should be wide. The second word should narrow the trail without naming the secret. Do not write the word itself, and do not name the deck. A table that hates typing can switch to spoken clues. The written trail is the sharper game, because the evidence is still there when you vote.

How many imposters

The stepper will not let the imposters outnumber the crew. Five to eight seats is the comfortable table. Three works, and it is brutal, because there is nowhere to hide.

A trail, then a vote

Suppose the word is kettle. A useful first pass is whistle, kitchen, metal, morning. A useless first pass is tea, boil, water, hot, because the imposter can already say kettle. On the vote, each person taps one slip. You may tap your own. The choice locks before the phone moves. If two slips tie for the most votes, nobody is out.

Questions

What do you write when you are the imposter?

Wait. Your first word should be as wide as the slips already on the table. Specific too early is how blank-card imposters get caught. On the second pass, match the level of detail the crew just used.

Can two people play?

No. With two people, one of you is the imposter and the other knows it. The game needs a third person to hide behind.

Who wins the imposter game?

The group wins by putting the only top vote on an imposter. The imposter wins by surviving that vote, or by naming the word after being caught.
