Play / Lookalike

Lookalike is the round you play once blank card feels familiar. The crew all see one word. The imposter sees a cousin of it, and the card does not say imposter. They will write clues for their word in good faith. The trail splits, and the vote is about which slip is describing the other thing.

The deal above is already in lookalike. Switch back to blank card on the table if you want the classic seal.

A wide clue such as hot, wet, or building keeps the imposter alive. That is legal. The second pass is where the table has to risk a detail. If nobody risks one, lookalike ends in a polite tie and the imposter slips away, which is the correct result.

Blank-card clue craft, for when you switch back, is on how to play.

A lookalike round keeps the written clues to remain even when they describe cousins, the vote is aimed at a slip, and one guess steals the round only by naming the crew word.

Questions

Does the imposter know they are the imposter in lookalike?

No. They see a normal word card. They find out from the other slips, or at the poster.

Can we mix lookalike with a single deck?

Lookalike uses the cousin list, not the supper or creatures deck. Pick the mode, not a deck.
