Play / One phone

This imposter game is built for one phone because a party dies while everyone installs something. The handset is the dealer. It names the next person, shows a card only while a thumb is down, and hides the word on the way to the next hand.

An imposter mobile game in an app store usually wants a phone per player so the secret can sit on a private screen. That is a fair way to play, and it is a bad way to start at a dinner where two people have no battery. Here the privacy is the hold. Let go, and the paper goes back to "Show my card" before you pass it. Crew cards and imposter cards are the same cream paper, so the color does not leak across the table.

What you do with the phone

Deal. Pass. Hold. Read. Let go. After every card has been seen, the phone becomes the table: two words each, pinned in order. Then it becomes the ballot. Then it becomes the poster, which you can copy into the group chat. The rules under that are on how to play.

If the group is loud and would rather speak, switch clues to spoken. You still only need the one phone for the deal and the vote.

Questions

Can the person next to me see the word?

Only if they are close enough to read the card while you are holding it. Let go before the phone leaves your hand.

Do we need one phone or one each?

One. That is the game on this page.
